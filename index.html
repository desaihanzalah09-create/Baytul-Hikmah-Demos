About Our Institution

Building Foundations. Mastering Knowledge. Pursuing Excellence.

Established in January 2026, Baytul-Hikmah Institution for Higher Islamic Education provides structured Islamic learning for students of all ages.

Our focus is to build strong foundations in the Islamic sciences, develop mastery of Arabic, and guide students towards advanced study with discipline, clarity, and love for lifelong learning.

Vision

To make serious Islamic education accessible, progressive, and rooted in authentic scholarship.

Mission

To equip students with Arabic, foundational Islamic sciences, sound understanding, and the tools for advanced study.

Approach

Active, Engaging Learning

Lessons encourage participation, memorisation, revision, questions, discussion, and regular assessment.

For All Ages

Flexible Study Pathways

Younger students may progress full-time, while adults can study selected subjects around work and family life.

Campus

Growing in Pietermaritzburg

Baytul-Hikmah begins with classroom, office, toilet, and bathroom facilities, with a long-term campus vision.

Madressa Glimpse

A Calm Space for Learning and Growth

Our Pietermaritzburg location offers classroom space, open grounds, and a peaceful setting as Baytul-Hikmah grows into a dedicated centre of Islamic education.

Academic Programmes

2027 pathways for male and female Alim students, full-time Hifz, and part-time Hifz learners

Part-Time

Part-Time Hifdh Al Quraan

Designed for students continuing memorisation with regular guided revision, Tajweed, and steady progress around their weekly commitments.

  • Saturday after Fajr for 2½ hours
  • Individualized progress tracking
  • Focus on Tajweed rules & retention
Full-Time

Full-Time Hifzhul Quraan

An immersive program focused on complete Quranic memorization, daily revision discipline, and mastery of recitation rules in an inspiring environment.

  • Morning session: 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m.
  • Afternoon session: 3:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m.
  • Structured revision (Sabaq, Sabqi, Dhor)
Advanced

Alim Course

A rigorous higher learning programme covering Classical Arabic, Qur'an recitation, Tajweed, Fiqh, Hadith, Tafseer, Islamic History, and Aqeedah.

  • Comprehensive Islamic Jurisprudence
  • Arabic Language & Literature
  • Deep study of primary Hadith texts
